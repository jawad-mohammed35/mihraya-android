# Mihraya 1.6.0 QA

November 15, 2026. Android 15/API 35 x86_64 emulator; no physical device.

## Scope and source checks

The library ships three source editions: Nawawi (42 records), Qudsi (40), and Dehlawi (40). English/Arabic packages total 183,334 uncompressed bytes. `tools/prepare_hadith.py` checks all included source IDs, Arabic/translation references, source numbering, section membership/counts, nonempty text and absence of U+FFFD. It preserves the supplied strings, order and numbering without textual repairs. All eight English/optional-language packages have pinned byte sizes and SHA-256 hashes. The source snapshot and the audit of excluded editions are recorded in `docs/hadith-source-audit.json`.

Bukhari, Muslim, Tirmidhi and the larger optional collections were excluded after damaged Arabic characters and/or blank records were found. These are not partial collections exposed as complete. Riyad was waived by the user because the suggested API does not supply it. Bulugh is absent from the source. Source equality and structural checks do not establish scholarly accuracy, canonical completeness across editions, or independent translator/publisher rights clearance. The upstream Unlicense and the outstanding rights distinction are documented in NOTICE, README, the in-app credits and reader source details. The user explicitly requested offline use of UmmahAPI's declared upstream after that limitation was disclosed.

## Limits

No physical device, Android 8/older API, OEM launcher, exhaustive original-feature end-to-end suite, long-duration download interruption/process-death simulation, or frame-time benchmark was run. Quran, audio and Tasbih implementations and their storage were left intact, with existing unit tests retained. The library currently has 122 records; large-corpus search and storage performance have not been claimed or benchmarked. Translation authorship and every translation's linguistic accuracy were not independently established. Similar-narration links and extracted isnad graphs are omitted because the source editions lack verified structured data.
